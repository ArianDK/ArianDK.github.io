Minecraft Backup

This is a personal backup utility for a Minecraft server on my Raspberry Pi. It makes a dated archive of the world when nobody is playing and uploads at most one backup per day to my Google Drive folder sync/minecraft. The upload runs after the game resumes.

The utility uses open-source rclone to access Google Drive. It is not a public backup service, and visitors cannot create accounts or upload files here. No world archives, sign-in tokens, or server credentials are hosted on this website.

Google Drive access

To work with a folder I created in advance, the Google authorization allows broad Drive access. The backup job is configured to find only the chosen folder, upload a world archive there, and check that the upload matches. I can revoke the authorization through my Google Account.
